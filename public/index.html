      How to play

      1. One player opens a room and sends the invite link. The other opens it on their own device.
      2. The Caller sees the inside of the exchange: symbols, lamps and machines. Only the Caller can work the machines.
      3. The Operator holds the field manual: what everything means, and the rules. Only the Operator can read it.
      4. Talk it through in the chat, or on a phone or voice call. Neither of you can solve a puzzle alone.
      5. Each stage is three puzzles. Restore it to earn up to three stars and open the next of 200 stages.

      Last Call keeps anonymous play statistics (which puzzles get solved, and how often connections drop) to improve the game. It never stores names, chat or room codes.
