How each number is counted
Players connected now
Seats with an open connection at this moment, counted from server memory. Live numbers reset when the server restarts.
Active rooms now
Rooms with at least one player connected. Open rooms also includes rooms waiting for players to come back.
Peak concurrent players
The most players connected at the same moment: since the last restart (live), and per UTC day (stored).
Stage started
Either player started a stage. One stage attempt is one “game”.
Won
All three puzzles of the stage were solved.
Lost
The stage countdown ran out (countdown rooms only).
Abandoned
The stage ended without a win or a loss: a player went back to the map, or the room closed. A stage with no result 6 hours after it started (for example, lost to a server restart) also counts as abandoned, on the day it started.
Completed
Won plus lost: stages that reached a result screen.
Completion rate
Won ÷ (won + lost + abandoned). Stages still in progress are left out.
Time ranges
Today is from 00:00 UTC; the last 7 and 30 days are rolling windows ending now. Results are counted when they happen, so a stage started before a range and won inside it adds to “won” but not “started”.
Browser session
One browser tab’s session: a random ID kept in the tab’s session storage, which ends when the tab is closed. The same person on two devices, or after closing the tab, counts again. These are browser sessions, not people.
Room session
One browser session’s time in one room, from first connecting to leaving or the room closing.
Average session duration
The mean length of room sessions that ended in the range. Sessions cut off by a server restart have no end and are left out.
Drops
A player’s connection closed without them leaving. Long drops lasted more than 8 seconds, the point at which the partner is told.
Drops per session
Drops ÷ room sessions joined in the range.
Quick recoveries
Reconnections within 8 seconds of a drop ÷ drops.
Puzzle completion
Puzzles solved ÷ puzzles shown, per machine. Wrong answers and hints are per puzzle shown; average attempts counts the answers sent for each solved puzzle.
Storage and retention
Each event is stored once, even if it is written twice. After the retention period, events are added up into daily totals and deleted.
